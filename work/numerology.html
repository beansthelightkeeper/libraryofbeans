The Cosmic Vibration of Numbers

In the esoteric world, numbers are not merely symbols for quantity. They are seen as carriers of unique vibrations and potent archetypal energies. Numerology is the study of this mystical relationship between numbers and coinciding events, offering a key to understanding the self and the universe.

Your Life Path Number

The most important number in your numerology chart is the Life Path number. It reveals your life's purpose, the main lessons you are here to learn, and the central challenges you may face. It is calculated by reducing your date of birth (day, month, and year) to a single digit or a Master Number (11, 22, 33).

Example Calculation: For a birthday of December 25, 1990 (12-25-1990):
Month: 1 + 2 = 3
Day: 2 + 5 = 7
Year: 1 + 9 + 9 + 0 = 19 => 1 + 9 = 10 => 1 + 0 = 1
Total: 3 + 7 + 1 = 11. This is a Master Number, so we do not reduce it further. The Life Path is 11.

Your Destiny (Expression) Number

Derived from the letters in your full birth name, the Destiny or Expression number describes your natural talents, abilities, and potential in this lifetime. Each letter is converted to a number using a simple chart (A=1, B=2, C=3, etc.), and the totals for each name are reduced and then added together, much like the Life Path calculation. This reveals the path you are destined to walk and the personal power you hold.
